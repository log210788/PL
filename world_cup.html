1. Grammar Guide: Prepositions of Time

Preposition Rule / When to Use Football Examples
AT Clock times, specific precise moments, holiday periods • The match starts at 3:00 PM.
• We scored a goal at kick-off.
ON Days of the week, calendar dates, parts of specific days • Italy lost their play-off on March 31st.
• Brazil plays Morocco on Saturday afternoon.
IN Months, years, seasons, decades, long durations • The World Cup runs in June and July.
• He was a star player in the 1990s.
DURING Within the duration of an event • Nobody scored during the first half.
BEFORE / AFTER Prior to / subsequent to an event • Warm up before the game.
• Shake hands after the final whistle.

🇮🇹 Italian Speaker Alert! (Evita questi errori!)

  • ❌ Don't say: "The match is in Sunday morning." ➡️ Say: "The match is **on** Sunday morning."
  • ❌ Don't say: "The final is in July 19th." ➡️ Say: "The final is **on** July 19th."
  • ❌ Don't say: "I will watch it at the evening." ➡️ Say: "I will watch it **in** the evening."

Exercise A: True or False?

1. Canada plays Bosnia and Herzegovina on Friday, June 12th at 3:00 PM.

Correct: True (Matches our Friday Group B schedule)

2. Italy is scheduled to play their first match on Sunday morning.

Correct: False (Italy did not qualify for the 2026 World Cup)

3. Switzerland plays Qatar on Saturday, June 13th.

Correct: True (They play in Group B on Saturday afternoon)

4. The World Cup final will take place during the month of August.

Correct: False (The final is on July 19th)

5. The Netherlands vs. Japan match starts at 3:00 PM on Sunday.

Correct: True (Group F, Dallas, kicks off at 3:00 PM on June 14th)

Exercise B: Fill in the Blanks

Choose the correct preposition: at, on, in, during, before, after.

1. The USA will play Paraguay ______ June 12th.

Correct: on

2. Brazil vs. Morocco kicks off ______ 4:00 PM on Saturday.

Correct: at

3. The entire World Cup tournament will be held ______ 2026.

Correct: in

4. The players stood silently ______ the national anthems before kick-off.

Correct: during

5. Fans should arrive at the stadium ______ the match starts to find their seats.

Correct: before

6. We celebrated with drinks ______ our team won the match.

Correct: after

Exercise C: Multiple Choice

1. Which preposition is correct for a specific day of the week, like Sunday?

Correct: C (on Sunday)

2. Which preposition is correct for a specific year, like 2026?

Correct: A (in 2026)
