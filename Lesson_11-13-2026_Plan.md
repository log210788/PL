## 🎯 Teaching Philosophy for This Lesson
Paolo is expressive and loves conversation, but Italian transfer shows up in three predictable spots: **dropped `/h/`**, **epenthetic vowel at the end of consonants** (*friends-uh*), and **short `/ɪ/` vs. long `/iː/`** (*live/leave*). Plus, idioms often trip on **prepositions** (`on the same page about`) and **gerunds** (`stop beating`).
- **Correction Rule:** During sound drills, be playful and physical (*hand in front of mouth for `/h/`*). During his 2-minute story, jot down preposition/gerund slips silently and fix them right after the story ends.

## ⏱️ Minute-by-Minute Teaching Script

### 1. Italian → English Sound Warm-Up (00:00 – 09:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 1.*
- **How to teach `/h/`:** *"Put your palm 5 cm in front of your mouth. When you say `heat`, `hit`, `habit`, you should feel a warm puff of air. When you say `eat` or `ear`, zero air!"* Drill: *eat/heat • ear/hear • ill/hill*.
- **How to teach Dry Final Consonants:** Italian ends most words in vowels, so learners add a tiny `-uh` (*friend-uh*, *beat-uh*). Tell Paolo: *"Freeze your tongue/lips on the last sound and cut the voice off!"* Drill: *friends, plates, habits, beats*.
- **How to teach `/ɪ/` vs. `/iː/`:** *"Short `/ɪ/` (`bit, live, sit`) is relaxed and quick—like a snap. Long `/iː/` (`beat, leave, seat`) stretches into a smile."* Test him blindly: say one word and have him tell you #1 (short) or #2 (long), then swap!

### 2. Target Idioms & Grammar Traps (09:00 – 23:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 2.*
- **Worksheet Part 2 Answer Key:** `1. play it by ear` • `2. beating around the bush` • `3. on the same page about` • `4. hit the ground running` • `5. easier said than done` • `6. bit off more than I could chew`.
- **How to teach it:**
  1. Don't just read definitions—ask Paolo to guess the visual image behind `bite off more than you can chew` and `beat around the bush`.
  2. Highlight the **2 grammar traps** right away:
     - **Trap 1:** `on the same page` + **`about`** (NOT *for* or *on* a topic).
     - **Trap 2:** `stop` + **`-ing`** → *"Stop **beating** around the bush"* (NOT *stop to beat*).
  3. Complete Worksheet Part 2 together aloud, checking tense (`hit` stays `hit` in past; `bite` → `bit`).

### 3. Core Activity: 2-Minute Storytelling Challenge (23:00 – 43:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 3.*
- **Setup:** *"Tell me a 2-minute story—real or completely invented—about cinema, a trip, or a chaotic week when plans changed at the last minute. Rule: you must sneak in at least **3** of today's idioms!"*
- **Your job while listening:** Keep a tally of the 3 idioms on screen (give a thumbs-up when he lands one!) and write down 2–3 sentences to polish afterward.
- **Follow-Up Rapid-Fire Questions (8 mins):** Keep him thinking on his feet without translating:
  - *"Did your friends agree on the plan, or were you not on the same page about it?"*
  - *"Next time that happens, will you plan ahead or just play it by ear?"*
  - *"Tell me honestly—did you bite off more than you could chew?"*
- **Debrief (4 mins):** Fix any missing `/h/`, trailing `-uh`, or preposition slips from his story, then have him repeat 2 corrected sentences smoothly.

### 4. Wrap-Up & Homework (43:00 – 50:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 4.*
- Share 3 fluency/pronunciation wins + 2 grammar/sound reminders.
- Assign 4 short written sentences using `on the same page about`, `stop beating around the bush`, `play it by ear`, and `bite off more than you can chew`.

## 🛟 Pacing Rescue & Extension
- **If he finishes the story in 45 seconds:** Ask *"Wait—what happened BEFORE that? And how did the other person react?"* to stretch his narrative tenses.
- **If time remains at the end:** Play "20-Second Hot Seat"—give him one idiom and he must start a sentence with it immediately.
