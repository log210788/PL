9-Lesson English Conversation & Idioms

A custom syllabus designed to target speaking fluency, vocabulary expansion, and structural corrections specifically tailored for an Italian speaker.

Lesson 1

Everyday Habits & Socializing

Conversation Focus: Discussing daily routines, lifestyles, hobbies, and social interactions with ease and confidence.

Target Idioms & Sayings

Hit the ground running
🇮🇹 Partire a pieno ritmo / Iniziare con energia
To start something quickly with energy and enthusiasm.
Keep at it
🇮🇹 Continua così / Non mollare
To continue trying or working hard at something.
Go the extra mile
🇮🇹 Fare il passo in più
To make more effort than expected or required.
Bite off more than you can chew
🇮🇹 Fare il passo più lungo della gamba
To try to do something too difficult for you.
Easier said than done
🇮🇹 Più facile a dirsi che a farsi
Something sounds simple but is much harder in practice.
On the same page
🇮🇹 Essere sulla stessa lunghezza d'onda
To have the same understanding about something.
Beat around the bush
🇮🇹 Menare il can per l'aia / Girare intorno
To avoid the main topic; to be indirect.
Get straight to the point
🇮🇹 Andare dritti al punto
To say clearly and directly what you mean.
👄 Speech Mechanics Target: Focus on pronouncing the breathy /h/ sound in habit, hobby, hit, and have. Avoid adding a quiet vowel (epenthesis) to final consonants in keep, point, and page.
Lesson 2

Planning Group Trips & Event Coordination

Conversation Focus: Discussing schedules with friends, coordinating arrivals, and deciding on travel plans together.

Target Idioms & Sayings

Back to the drawing board
🇮🇹 Ricominciare da capo
To start planning over because the previous plan failed.
Cut corners
🇮🇹 Prendere scorciatoie / Risparmiare sulla qualità
To do something in the easiest, cheapest, or fastest way (often dropping quality).
Learn the ropes
🇮🇹 Imparare come fare qualcosa
To learn the basics of how a new hobby or activity is done.
In the loop
🇮🇹 Tenere informato / Al corrente
To be kept informed about travel updates or social plans.
By the book
🇮🇹 Secondo le regole
Doing something strictly according to rules or guidelines (like playing board games).
Touch base
🇮🇹 Contattare brevemente / Sentirsi
To make brief contact with a friend to catch up or coordinate.
Put on hold
🇮🇹 Mettere in pausa / Sospendere
To delay or pause an activity or plan temporarily.
Call it a day
🇮🇹 Finire per oggi / Concludere un'attività
To stop doing an activity for the rest of the day.
👄 Speech Mechanics Target: Contrast the final voiceless /s/ and voiced /z/ sounds. Practice linking the final consonants to vowels seamlessly: "Call-it-a-day".
Lesson 3

Making Plans & Reaching Agreement with Friends

Conversation Focus: Deciding on weekend activities, splitting trip expenses, and planning group dinners.

Target Idioms & Sayings

See eye to eye
🇮🇹 Essere d'accordo
To agree fully with someone.
Play devil's advocate
🇮🇹 Fare l'avvocato del diavolo
To present an opposing view for the sake of argument.
A penny for your thoughts
🇮🇹 A cosa stai pensando?
A way of asking what someone is thinking about.
Elephant in the room
🇮🇹 Un problema evidente ignorato
An obvious problem that people avoid talking about.
Take it with a grain of salt
🇮🇹 Prenderlo con le pinze
To listen to information skeptically; not taking it literally.
Break the ice
🇮🇹 Rompere il ghiaccio
To relieve tension or initiate social interaction.
Close, but no cigar
🇮🇹 Quasi, ma non del tutto
Very near to success but not quite succeeding.
No pain, no gain
🇮🇹 Nessun risultato senza sforzo
You must work hard and suffer to make progress.
👄 Speech Mechanics Target: Focus on clear alveolar nasal /n/ endings in pain and gain without appending extra vowel clicks.
Lesson 4

Busy Schedules & Traveling

Conversation Focus: Discussing busy weeks, coordinating timings, and making it to events in the nick of time.

Target Idioms & Sayings

Against the clock
🇮🇹 Corsa contro il tempo
Working with a very strict limit of time.
Down to the wire
🇮🇹 All'ultimo minuto / All'ultimo secondo
Up to the very last possible moment.
In the nick of time
🇮🇹 Giusto in tempo
Just at the last possible moment.
Burn the midnight oil
🇮🇹 Fare le ore piccole per lavorare/studiare
To study or work late into the night.
Once in a blue moon
🇮🇹 Una volta ogni morte di papa
Very rarely.
Out of hand
🇮🇹 Sfuggire di mano / Fuori controllo
Out of control.
On thin ice
🇮🇹 Essere in una situazione rischiosa
To be in a risky position or situation.
Under the weather
🇮🇹 Non sentirsi bene / Giù di corda
Slightly sick or unwell.
👄 Speech Mechanics Target: Slide smoothly into initial consonant clusters like /st/ or /sp/ (e.g. stress, speak) without inserting an initial "e" or "i" sound.
Lesson 5

Decision Making & Choosing Activities

Conversation Focus: Deciding on options, making choices with friends, and facing the consequences of small errors.

Target Idioms & Sayings

The ball is in your court
🇮🇹 La palla passa a te / Tocca a te decidere
It is your responsibility to make the next decision or step.
Call the shots
🇮🇹 Dettare legge / Prendere le decisioni
To make the decisions or be in charge.
Sit on the fence
🇮🇹 Essere indeciso / Non prendere posizione
To avoid making a decision or choice.
Throw caution to the wind
🇮🇹 Gettare la prudenza al vento / Rischiare
To take a huge risk or act without worry.
Up in the air
🇮🇹 Ancora incerto / In sospeso
Unresolved or undecided.
Take a rain check
🇮🇹 Rimandare a un'altra volta
Politely decline an invitation now, but suggest doing it later.
Miss the boat
🇮🇹 Perdere l'occasione
To miss an opportunity.
Face the music
🇮🇹 Affrontare le conseguenze
To accept criticism or consequences for actions.
👄 Speech Mechanics Target: Practice contrasting long vs. short English vowels. Ensure sit (/sɪt/) does not sound like seat (/siːt/) and miss (/mɪs/) does not sound like mees.
Lesson 6

Sharing Stories & Solving Everyday Glitches

Conversation Focus: Discussing past trips, describing funny mishaps, and resolving everyday household issues.

Target Idioms & Sayings

Cry over spilled milk
🇮🇹 Piangere sul latte versato
To worry about past mistakes that cannot be changed.
Bark up the wrong tree
🇮🇹 Prendere una strada sbagliata / Rivolgersi a chi non c'entra
To pursue a line of thought or action that is misguided.
Blow off steam
🇮🇹 Sfogarsi / Scaricare la tensione
To do something to get rid of stress or anger.
Cross that bridge when we come to it
🇮🇹 Affrontare il problema quando si presenterà
To deal with a problem when it happens, not before.
Spill the beans
🇮🇹 Sputare il rospo / Svelare il segreto
To reveal secret information.
Piece of cake
🇮🇹 Un gioco da ragazzi
Something very easy to do.
A blessing in disguise
🇮🇹 Una fortuna sfacciata / Un male per un bene
Something that seems bad at first but turns out well.
Best of both worlds
🇮🇹 Il meglio di due mondi
Enjoying the advantages of two different situations at once.
👄 Speech Mechanics Target: Focus on the voiceless /θ/ sound (e.g. think, through) rather than substituting it with /t/ or /s/.
Lesson 7

Making Friends & Social Gatherings

Conversation Focus: Starting conversations with new neighbors, introducing friends, and talking about local spots.

Target Idioms & Sayings

Break a leg
🇮🇹 In bocca al lupo
A superstitious way to wish someone good luck.
Costs an arm and a leg
🇮🇹 Costa un occhio della testa
Very expensive.
A dime a dozen
🇮🇹 Molto comune / Facile da trovare
Very common and not of much value.
Through thick and thin
🇮🇹 Nella buona e nella cattiva sorte
Through all difficulties and good times.
Add insult to injury
🇮🇹 Oltre al danno la beffa
To make a bad situation even worse.
Hear it through the grapevine
🇮🇹 Venir a sapere per via indiretta
To hear rumors or gossip from informal sources.
Live and learn
🇮🇹 Sbagliando si impara
To learn from your errors or experiences.
So far, so good
🇮🇹 Fino a qui tutto bene
Things have gone well up to this point.
👄 Speech Mechanics Target: Focus on English syllable stress patterns in multi-syllable words and link final consonants in fast speech.
Lesson 8

Storytelling & Telling Funny Tales

Conversation Focus: Recounting stories to a group, sharing personal passions, and giving speeches at a celebration.

Target Idioms & Sayings

Get the ball rolling
🇮🇹 Dare inizio alle danze / Avviare
To start a process or activity.
Draw the line
🇮🇹 Porre un limite
To set a limit on what you will allow or do.
Keep your chin up
🇮🇹 Tieni duro / Non abbatterti
To remain cheerful and hopeful in a difficult situation.
Think outside the box
🇮🇹 Pensare fuori dagli schemi
To think creatively, using unconventional methods.
Pull yourself together
🇮🇹 Riprenditi / Datti un contegno
To regain control of your emotions and act calmly.
Burn bridges
🇮🇹 Tagliare i ponti
To destroy relationships or pathways back.
Actions speak louder than words
🇮🇹 I fatti contano più delle parole
What you do has a stronger impact than what you say.
A penny for your thoughts
🇮🇹 Cosa ti passa per la testa?
Asking for someone's opinion or thoughts.
👄 Speech Mechanics Target: Sustain continuous breath flow and avoid dropping final consonants (e.g. think, keep, pitch).
Lesson 9

Capstone Social Simulation

Conversation Focus: Sharing a 3-minute story about a past trip or hobby, integrating target idioms naturally in a social conversation.

👄 Capstone Speech Challenge: Paolo will share a 3-minute story about a memorable trip or a hobby. He must use at least 4 idioms from the curriculum naturally, demonstrating clear consonant releases, breathy /h/ sounds, proper preposition choices, and correct stress timing.
