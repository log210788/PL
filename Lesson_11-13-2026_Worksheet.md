**Topic:** Storytelling & Everyday Idioms Under Pressure

## Part 1: Sound Contrast Warm-Up (Italian → English)
Read each pair out loud — keep final consonants **dry** (no extra `-uh` at the end!):
1. **Aspirated `/h/` vs. no `/h/`:** *eat / heat* • *ear / hear* • *ill / hill* • *ahead / hobby / habit*
2. **Short `/ɪ/` vs. Long `/iː/`:** *bit / beat* • *live / leave* • *sit / seat* • *fit / feet*
3. **Dry final consonants:** *friends • plates • habits • beats • plans*

---

## Part 2: Idiom Match & Grammar Watch
Choose the best idiom for each situation:
`hit the ground running` | `bite off more than you can chew` | `easier said than done` | `on the same page about` | `beat around the bush` | `play it by ear`

1. *"We don't have a fixed schedule for Saturday yet — let's just _______________________."*
2. *"Stop _______________________ and tell me what actually happened!"* *(Note: gerund `-ing` after stop!)*
3. *"Before we book the flights, let's make sure we're _______________________ the budget."* *(Watch: preposition **about**!)*
4. *"She joined the team on Monday and _______________________ — she solved three bugs before lunch."*
5. *"Staying calm when your train is cancelled is _______________________."*
6. *"I agreed to organize two events in one weekend — I definitely _______________________."*
