Candidate Task Card
Describe a pivotal decision you made that significantly influenced your life.

You should say:

  • What the decision was and when you made it
  • What other alternatives you considered
  • Why you decided to make that choice
  • And explain how you feel about the outcome of that decision today

✍️ Writing Task 2 Diagnostic Workshop

Compare these two candidate body paragraphs responding to the prompt: *"Do the benefits of remote work outweigh the drawbacks?"* **Hover or click on the highlighted terms** in the Band 7 essay to see the grammatical and lexical features that make it superior.

IELTS Prompt: "In many countries, an increasing number of people are working from home. Do the advantages of this trend outweigh the disadvantages?"

Candidate Response Band 6.0

On the one hand, working from home has some good benefits. First, employees do not have to travel to work every day. This saves a lot of time and money that people spend on trains and fuel. In addition, workers can have a flexible schedule. They can work when they want, so they can take care of their children and clean the house easily. Consequently, this makes staff feel happier and less stressed in their daily lives.
Tutor Analysis: Uses basic vocabulary (*"good benefits"*, *"saves a lot of time"*, *"take care of"*). Sentence structure is repetitive (mostly simple Subject-Verb-Object). Connectors are standard (*"First"*, *"In addition"*, *"Consequently"*).

Candidate Response Band 7.5

Undeniably, telecommuting offers several profound advantages. Chief among these is the elimination of daily commutes, which significantly reduces transportation costs and recovers valuable hours. Furthermore, flexible working arrangements, which allow employees to coordinate tasks around family commitments, foster a healthier work-life balance. Consequently, this autonomy not only enhances job satisfaction but also mitigates work-related stress.
Tutor Analysis: Features high-level synonyms (*"telecommuting"*, *"mitigates"*). Contains complex grammar (non-defining relative clauses, passive voice, nominalization, correlative structures). Cohesion is varied and smooth.
