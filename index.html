🩺 Paolo's Grammar, Pronunciation & False Friends Clinic

💬 1. "Talk to" vs. "Talk with" (Your Question!)

✅ "Tomorrow I'm going to uni to talk to a lot of people." (Default in everyday UK/US English)
✅ "I enjoy talking with my friends over coffee." (Highlights mutual two-way exchange)

Both are completely correct! You never need to worry about choosing between them, though talk to is slightly more common in British English.

⚠️ 2. False Friend Alert: "Eventually"

❌ Italian "eventualmente" = possibly / if necessary
✅ English "eventually" = Alla fine / Finalmente (after a long delay)

From today: "The traffic takes forever, so I work at the office and go home eventually at the end of the day."

🧠 3. Natural Word Choice Upgrades

❌ "My father told me no drugs — a good tradition." / "Change the mental list."
✅ "My father gave me good advice." / "Change their mentality."

Remember: advice is uncountable (never an advice), and we buy a pack of 20 cigarettes (rather than a box).
