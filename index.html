Part 1 • Practical Application

5 Real-Life Scenarios: Use Your New Idioms

Write 1–2 natural sentences for each scenario using the target idiom from today's lesson:

5 Tasks
Scenario 1 • Extreme Happiness • Target: "on cloud nine" Italian parallel: Siamo al settimo cielo

You just booked your family holiday to Japan, or received exciting news at work. How do you describe how you feel?

Scenario 2 • Hesitation & Doubt • Target: "on the fence" Meaning: undecided between two choices

A friend asks whether you prefer working entirely from home or renting an office in the city center. You see pros and cons in both.

Scenario 3 • Strong Agreement • Target: "you can say that again" Meaning: I completely agree with you

A colleague says: "Traffic on a rainy Monday morning takes forever!" Reply with strong enthusiasm.

Scenario 4 • Staying Composed • Target: "stiff upper lip" Meaning: be strong, don't panic, keep going

Describe a time when family life was loud or work deadlines were stressful, and how you managed to stay calm and keep going.

Scenario 5 • Warm Weekend Sign-Off • Target: "stay out of trouble" Friendly parting advice

You are finishing a call on Friday evening with a close friend or colleague who is heading out for the weekend. How do you sign off?
