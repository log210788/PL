Going to the Cinema

Read the dialogue scenario, review the core vocabulary terms, and complete the interactive exercises. Don't forget to export your PDF answers and send them to your tutor!

1. Core Vocabulary

Feel like Avere voglia di

Want to do or watch something right now.

"I feel like watching a comedy tonight."

Boring / Slow Noioso / Lento

Not exciting; nothing happens in the movie.

"That movie is too slow. I prefer action."

Hit movie Film di successo

A very popular and successful movie.

"The new dinosaur film is a huge hit movie."

Showtime Orario spettacolo

The exact time the movie starts at the theater.

"What showtime do you want? 7:00 or 9:00?"

Full / Sold out Tutto esaurito

No more tickets left. All seats are taken.

"We can't go. The theater is full."

Front row Prima fila

The very first line of seats, close to the screen.

"I don't like the front row. It hurts my neck."

2. The Scenario: "At the Movie Theater"

Sam

Hi Alex! We are finally here. What do you feel like watching?

Alex

I'm not sure. There is a new superhero movie, or that drama about the doctor.

Sam

I think the drama looks a bit slow and boring for a Friday night. Let's watch the superhero one! It's a massive hit movie.

Alex

Sounds good. What showtime is best?

Sam

There is one starting in 5 minutes, or one at 8:00 PM.

Alex

5 minutes is too fast. We need time to buy popcorn. Let's do the 8:00 PM one.

Sam

Oh wait, the 8:00 PM showtime is almost full. There are only two tickets left, and they are in the front row.

Alex

Oh no, I hate the front row! It's too close to the screen. Is there another movie?

Sam

Yes, there is a scary horror movie at 8:15 PM. It has great seats in the middle.

Alex

Perfect. Let's buy the tickets for that one!

3. Student Exercises

Exercise A: True or False?

1. Sam wants to watch the drama movie.

Correct: False (Sam thinks it looks slow and boring)

2. The superhero movie is very popular.

Correct: True (Sam calls it a massive hit movie)

3. They choose the movie that starts in 5 minutes.

Correct: False (They need time to buy popcorn and choose 8:00 PM)

4. Alex dislikes sitting in the front row.

Correct: True (Alex says: "I hate the front row! It's too close to the screen")

5. They decide to buy tickets for a horror movie.

Correct: True (They buy tickets for the horror movie at 8:15 PM)

Exercise B: Fill in the Blanks

1. I don't want to see that film. It looks very ______. I want something exciting!

Hint: Not exciting; nothing happens.
Correct: boring / slow

2. The movie starts at 7:30 PM. That is the perfect ______ for us.

Hint: The time the movie starts.
Correct: showtime

3. I am tired tonight. I just ______ watching a comedy at home.

Hint: Want to watch something right now.
Correct: feel like

4. We cannot buy tickets for the Friday show anymore. The theater is ______.

Hint: No more tickets left.
Correct: full / sold out

5. If you sit in the ______, you might have to stretch your neck to see.

Hint: The very first line of seats.
Correct: front row

6. The new action block-buster is a huge ______. It has earned millions!

Hint: A very popular and successful movie.
Correct: hit movie

Exercise C: Meaning Match & Written Prompt

1. What does it mean when a movie is "sold out"?

Correct: B

2. If you want to know what time a film starts, what do you look for?

Correct: A
