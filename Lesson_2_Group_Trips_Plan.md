## 🎯 Teaching Focus for This Lesson
Moving to **Curriculum Lesson 2**: coordinating group trips, handling changing plans, and communicating clearly with friends.
- **Pronunciation Focus (New!):**
  1. **`/s/` vs. `/z/` endings:** Italian speakers often devoice final `/z/` into `/s/` (*plans*, *corners*, *friends*).
  2. **Smooth Consonant Linking (No `-uh`!):** Linking final consonants directly into the next vowel (*call-it-a-day*, *put-it-on-hold*) instead of inserting an epenthetic `-uh`.
- **Grammar & Preposition Traps:**
  - `touch base` + **`with`** (person) + **`about`** (topic)
  - `keep someone` + **`in`** `the loop` (NOT *inside* or *on*)
  - `put something` + **`on`** `hold` (NOT *in* hold)

## ⏱️ Minute-by-Minute Teaching Script

### 1. Sound Warm-Up: `/s/` vs. `/z/` & Linking (00:00 – 09:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 1.*
- **How to teach `/s/` vs. `/z/`:** *"Put two fingers gently on your throat. Say `sssss` (like a snake — no vibration). Now say `zzzzz` (like a bee — feel the buzz!)."*
  - **Voiceless `/s/` (no buzz):** *ropes, tickets, trips, seats*
  - **Voiced `/z/` (buzz!):** *corners, plans, friends, hotels, trains*
- **How to teach Linking:** *"Instead of stopping and adding `-uh` (`call-uh it-uh a day`), glide straight into the next word like one smooth word:"*
  - *call it a day* → **call-i-ta-day**
  - *put it on hold* → **pu-ti-ton-hold**
  - *back to the* → **back-tə-the**

### 2. Target Idioms & Preposition Traps (09:00 – 23:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 2.*
- **Worksheet Part 2 Answer Key:** `1. in the loop` • `2. touch base` • `3. cut corners` • `4. put on hold` • `5. back to the drawing board` • `6. call it a day`.
- **How to teach it:**
  1. Ask Paolo to guess the meaning of **cut corners** and **back to the drawing board** before revealing the definitions.
  2. Highlight the **3 preposition traps**:
     - *"Let's **touch base with** Marco **about** the hotel."*
     - *"Keep me **in** the loop!"*
     - *"We had to **put** the trip **on** hold."*
  3. Complete Worksheet Part 2 aloud together, checking linking and `/z/` endings (*cut corner**z***).

### 3. Core Activity: Group Trip Roleplay & Crisis Negotiation (23:00 – 43:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 3.*
- **Setup:** *"You and 4 friends are planning a 4-day road trip (or weekend getaway). Suddenly, 3 things go wrong: the rental car company cancels, two friends haven't replied, and someone wants to book a super cheap, sketchy hotel to save money."*
- **Task (2 rounds):**
  - **Round 1 (Voice Message / Call to a Friend — 2 mins):** Paolo leaves a voice update for the group leader using at least **3–4 idioms** from today.
  - **Round 2 (Live Debate — 8 mins):** You play the friend who wants to *cut corners* and book the cheap hotel. Paolo has to convince you to go *back to the drawing board* or *put things on hold* until everyone is *in the loop*.
- **Debrief (4 mins):** Polish 2–3 sentences from the roleplay (focusing on prepositions and `/s/` vs. `/z/` endings) and have Paolo repeat them smoothly.

### 4. Wrap-Up & Homework (43:00 – 50:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 4.*
- Share 3 wins + 2 pronunciation/preposition reminders.
- Assign 4 short sentences using `in the loop`, `touch base with ... about`, `put on hold`, and `back to the drawing board`.

---

## 🛟 Pacing Rescue & Extension
- **If the roleplay wraps up early:** Ask Paolo about a real trip or event he organized where plans fell through and they had to go *back to the drawing board*.
- **Bonus Idioms (if he breezes through all 6):** Introduce **by the book** (*following all the rules*) and **learn the ropes** (*learn how something works*).
