🩺 Paolo's Grammar, Pronunciation & False Friends Clinic

Key questions you asked today and natural upgrades for Italian native speakers.

💬 1. "Talk to" vs. "Talk with" (Your Question!)

✅ "Tomorrow I'm going to uni to talk to a lot of people." (Most common in UK/US)
✅ "I love talking with my friends over coffee." (Emphasizes mutual 2-way conversation)

Rule: Both are 100% grammatically correct! In everyday British English, talk to is the default choice in casual speech, while talk with highlights a collaborative two-way exchange.

⚠️ 2. False Friend Alert: "Eventually"

❌ Italian false friend: "Eventualmente" = possibly / if necessary
✅ English "Eventually" = Alla fine / Finalmente (after a long time)

From today's lesson: "The traffic takes forever, so I work at the shared office and go home eventually at the end of the day." (For Italian eventualmente, use possibly or if needed).

🧠 3. Word Choice Upgrades from Today

❌ "My father told me no drugs — this is a good tradition."
✅ "My father told me 'no drugs' — that was really good advice."
❌ "They need to change the mental list." / "A box of 20 cigarettes."
✅ "They need to change their mentality." / "A pack of 20 cigarettes."

Remember: Advice is uncountable (say good advice or a piece of advice, never an advice).

🗣️ 4. Second Conditional & Pronunciation

❌ "If I were in this place, I probably think about my life..."
✅ "If I were in this place, I would probably think about my life and my faults."

Pronunciation Gems:
• Pews /pjuːz/ (church benches)
• Priest /priːst/ (long /iː/, not priced)
• Shepherd /ˈʃepəd/ (the h is completely silent!)
