### 🩺 Quick Grammar & False Friends Clinic
- **`Talk to` vs. `Talk with`:** Both are grammatically correct! **Talk to** is the everyday default in British English (*"I'll talk to lots of people tomorrow"*), while **talk with** emphasizes a mutual two-way conversation.
- **⚠️ False Friend `Eventually`:** Means **alla fine / finalmente** (after a long time), **NOT** *eventualmente* (which is *possibly* or *if needed* in English).
- **Uncountable `Advice`:** Say *"My father gave me good advice"* (never *"an advice"* or *"a good tradition"* when meaning advice).
- **2nd Conditional:** *"If I **were** in this place, I **would** probably think about my life and my faults."*
