# Worksheet — Paolo (Lesson 2: Group Trips & Coordination)
**Topic:** Planning Group Trips & Event Coordination

---

## Part 1: Sound Contrast Warm-Up (`/s/` vs. `/z/` & Linking)
Read each group out loud. Keep your fingers lightly on your throat to feel the difference:
1. **Voiceless `/s/` (no buzz):** *ropes • tickets • trips • seats • flights*
2. **Voiced `/z/` (throat buzz!):** *corners • plans • friends • hotels • trains*
3. **Smooth Linking (glide into the next word, no `-uh`):**
   - *Let's **call it a day**.* (`call-i-ta-day`)
   - *We need to **put it on hold**.* (`pu-ti-ton-hold`)

---

## Part 2: Idiom Match & Preposition Watch
Choose the best idiom for each situation:
`touch base` | `in the loop` | `cut corners` | `put on hold` | `back to the drawing board` | `call it a day`

1. *"Please CC me on the emails with the hotel so I stay _______________________."* *(Watch: preposition **in**!)*
2. *"I'll _______________________ with Marco tomorrow about the train times."* *(Watch: **with** + person, **about** + topic!)*
3. *"If we _______________________ on the rental car, we might break down in the mountains."*
4. *"Since Giulia broke her leg, we decided to _______________________ our ski trip _______________________ until next winter."* *(Watch: **on** hold!)*
5. *"The airline cancelled our flight and the ferry is sold out — it's _______________________."*
6. *"We've been comparing Airbnb listings for two hours! Let's _______________________ and decide tomorrow."*

## Part 3: Group Trip Crisis Roleplay
You and 4 friends are organizing a 4-day getaway, and everything is going wrong:
- The rental car cancelled.
- Two friends haven't answered the group chat.
- One friend wants to book a super cheap, poorly rated hotel to save money.

**Your Challenge:**
1. **Voice Update (2 mins):** Leave a quick update for the group using **at least 3 idioms** from Part 2.
2. **Live Negotiation:** Convince your friend not to *cut corners* and agree on a backup plan.
