## Block 1: Sound Warm-Up (`/s/` vs `/z/` & Linking)
```text
🎯 TODAY'S GOAL: Planning Group Trips & Handling Changing Plans

🔊 SOUND WARM-UP (Feel the throat buzz!):
• Snake /s/ (no buzz): ropes • tickets • trips • seats
• Bee /z/ (buzz!): corners • plans • friends • hotels • trains
• Smooth Linking (no "-uh"!):
  - call it a day → "call-i-ta-day"
  - put it on hold → "pu-ti-ton-hold"
```

---

## Block 2: 6 Target Idioms
```text
🧠 TODAY'S IDIOMS
1. Touch base (with X about Y) — check in briefly for an update
2. In the loop — kept informed and updated (⚠️ "keep me IN the loop")
3. Cut corners — take cheap shortcuts that lower quality
4. Put on hold — pause or delay a plan (⚠️ "put it ON hold")
5. Back to the drawing board — start planning all over again
6. Call it a day — stop working/planning and finish for today
```
